# PART 14 — OLYMPIC WEIGHTLIFTING

## Why it's separate from powerlifting

Powerlifting is a strength sport with three slow lifts. Weightlifting is a **power and technique sport** with two fast ones — the snatch and the clean & jerk. Peak power output in a snatch is roughly triple that of a maximal deadlift. The limiter for most of your first two years isn't strength, it's **positions, timing, and overhead mobility.** You cannot muscle a snatch, and trying is how people conclude the sport isn't for them.

## The universal technical model

**Both lifts share the same skeleton:**

1. **Setup** — bar over mid-foot, shoulders slightly ahead of bar, lats tight, arms long and relaxed.
2. **First pull (floor → knee)** — slow and patient. Back angle stays *constant*. The bar moves back toward you as the knees clear.
3. **Transition / double knee bend** — knees re-bend under the bar as the torso comes vertical. This is the invisible part that makes everything else work.
4. **Second pull (power position → extension)** — violent, vertical, hips into the bar. Arms stay straight until the hips finish.
5. **Turnover/receipt** — pull yourself *under* the bar aggressively. The third pull is an active movement, not a fall.
6. **Recovery** — stand it up under control.

**The three cues that fix most faults:** *slow off the floor, finish the extension, pull under fast.*

## 14A. Beginner: Positions & Technique

**Who it's for:** new to the lifts, regardless of general strength.
**Goal by Week 12:** technically sound snatch and clean & jerk from the floor, established (light) 1RMs, overhead squat depth.
**Frequency:** 3 days/wk. **All loads stay light — 50–75% of a technically clean max. You're building a motor pattern, not a number.**

### Day 1 — Snatch
| Movement | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Overhead squat | 5 × 5 (start with a bar/PVC) |
| Snatch from power position (high hang) | 5 × 3 |
| Snatch from knee | 4 × 3 |
| Snatch pull | 4 × 3 @ 80% of best snatch |
| Front squat | 4 × 5 @ RPE 7 |
| Overhead/shoulder mobility | 10 min |

### Day 2 — Clean & Jerk
| Movement | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Tall clean / muscle clean | 4 × 3 (technique) |
| Clean from power position | 5 × 3 |
| Push press → power jerk → split jerk | 5 × 3 each progression tier |
| Clean pull | 4 × 3 |
| Back squat | 4 × 5 @ RPE 7 |
| Core: hollow, back extension | 3 sets each |

### Day 3 — Full lifts & strength
| Movement | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Snatch from floor | 6 × 2 @ 65–75% |
| Clean & jerk from floor | 6 × 1+1 @ 65–75% |
| Snatch balance | 4 × 3 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 |
| Pull-up | 3 × AMRAP |
| Ankle/wrist/thoracic mobility | 10 min |

### 12-week arc

| Wks | Focus | Load |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Positions only: power position → hang → floor. Never lift from the floor before the hang is clean. | Bar to 50% |
| **4** | **Deload** — PVC and empty bar technique, squats at 65% | — |
| 5–7 | Full lifts from the floor. Add the split jerk. Squat progression begins. | 60–70% |
| **8** | **Deload** | — |
| 9–11 | Introduce singles at 75–85%. Two attempts at a technically clean heavier lift per week. | 70–85% |
| 12 | **TEST: max snatch, max clean & jerk, max front squat** — stop at the first technical failure, not the first missed lift. | Max |

### Beginner rules
- **Film every working set.** Front and 45° side. There is no substitute — you cannot feel a bar path.
- **Miss forward = you jumped forward or pulled early with the arms. Miss backward = you slammed the hips instead of finishing vertical.**
- **Get a coach for at least 4 sessions if it's possible at all.** The skill acquisition curve here is steeper than in any other modality in this library. Twelve weeks with a coach beats twelve months without.
- **Mobility gates the sport:** ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic extension, shoulder flexion, and wrist extension. Run Part 10A alongside this program — it is not optional here.

## 14B. Advanced: Competition Peak

**Who it's for:** established lifter with reliable technique and known maxes, competing or testing at Week 12.
**Frequency:** 5 days, 8–12 sessions, classic lifts most days.
**Method:** high frequency, wave-loaded percentages, squat volume tapering into the peak.

### Block map

| Wks | Block | Classic lift % | Squat volume | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Volume/strength** | 70–80% for 2–3s | High: 5 × 5 @ 75–82% | Pulls, positional variations, squat strength |
| **4** | Deload | 65% singles | 3 × 5 @ 70% | — |
| 5–7 | **Intensity build** | 80–88% for 1–2s | Moderate: 5 × 3 @ 82–88% | Complexes, heavy pulls, competition timing |
| **8** | Deload | 70% | 3 × 3 @ 75% | — |
| 9–11 | **Peaking** | 88–100%+ singles | Low: 3 × 2 @ 88–92%, then singles | Competition simulation, openers, minimal variation |
| 12 | **Taper/meet** | Openers only, then compete | 2 × 2 @ 80% early week | Sharpness, weigh-in, timing |

### Variation and accessory selection

| Fault | Prescribed variation |
|---|---|
| Early arm bend | Snatch/clean pull with pause below knee; halting deadlift |
| Bar swings forward | Snatch from blocks at knee; slow-pull snatch with 3 s to knee |
| Weak turnover | Snatch balance, tall snatch, drop snatch, high-hang snatch |
| Missing jerks forward | Jerk from rack with pause in the dip; jerk recovery; pressing in split |
| Weak out of the hole | Front squat pause 3 s; heavy front squat singles |
| Weak off the floor | Deficit clean pulls; halting deadlifts @ 100–110% |

**Accessories, 2–3 per session, 3 × 8–12:** back extensions, barbell rows, strict press, RDLs, ab wheel. Cut entirely from Week 10.

### Meet-week execution
Openers should be a weight you can hit **on your worst day, cold, three times out of three** — typically 88–90% of your best. Second attempt: your recent training best. Third: a small PR. Six lifts, six makes, is a better meet than three makes and a PR.

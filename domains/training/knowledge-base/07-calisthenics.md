# PART 7 — CALISTHENICS

## The two axes of bodyweight strength

Calisthenics progresses on **leverage**, not load. Two distinct qualities:

- **Bent-arm strength** — pull-ups, dips, muscle-ups, handstand push-ups. Trains like normal strength work.
- **Straight-arm strength** — front lever, planche, iron cross. Trains connective tissue and elbow/shoulder isometric capacity. **Adapts far more slowly than muscle** — this is where injuries come from rushing.

**Skill work goes first in every session, at full freshness, well short of failure.** You cannot practice a skill while fatigued; you can only practice doing it badly.

---

## 7A. Beginner: First Pull-Up, Dip, and Handstand Line

**Who it's for:** can't yet do 5 strict pull-ups, or is new to bodyweight training.
**Goal by Week 12:** 5–8 strict pull-ups, 8+ dips, 20+ push-ups, a solid wall handstand, working pistol progression.
**Frequency:** 4 days full body (Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri).

### Progression ladders

| Target | Ladder (advance when you hit the top of the rep range for all sets) |
|---|---|
| **Pull-up** | Dead hang 30 s → scapular pull 3×8 → inverted row (feet elevated) 3×10 → band-assisted 3×6 → negative 3×5 (5 s lower) → **strict pull-up** |
| **Push-up** | Wall → incline 3×12 → knee 3×12 → full 3×8 → diamond/deficit 3×8 → archer 3×5 |
| **Dip** | Bench dip 3×12 → band-assisted parallel bar 3×8 → negative 3×5 → **full dip** 3×5 → weighted |
| **Handstand** | Wall plank (chest to wall) 3×30 s → wall handstand 3×30 s → chest-to-wall 3×45 s → wall taps 3×8 → freestanding |
| **Pistol squat** | Box squat to bench 3×8 → assisted (TRX/rig) 3×6 → elevated-heel pistol 3×3 → **full pistol** |
| **L-sit** | Foot-supported tuck 3×20 s → tuck hang 3×15 s → one-leg 3×10 s → **L-sit** 3×10 s |

### Session template (each of 4 days)

1. **Skill — 10 min** (handstand or L-sit), fresh, low fatigue, stop before form breaks
2. **Pull — 3–4 sets** at your current ladder rung, 2 RIR
3. **Push — 3–4 sets**
4. **Legs — 3 sets**
5. **Core — 2–3 sets** (hollow body hold, hanging knee raise, side plank)

### 12-week arc

| Wks | Sets/movement | Proximity to failure | Add |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 3 | 3 RIR | Establish ladder rungs; log every set |
| **4** | 2 | 4 RIR | **Deload** |
| 5–7 | 4 | 2 RIR | Add tempo (3 s eccentrics) |
| **8** | 2 | 4 RIR | **Deload** |
| 9–11 | 4–5 | 1 RIR | Advance a rung wherever possible; add a weighted vest to easy rungs |
| 12 | 2 | 4 RIR | **Deload + retest maxes** |

### Wrist and elbow prep (non-negotiable — do it every session)
Wrist circles, front/back leaning wrist stretches 30 s each, finger pulses 2 × 15, and **elbow-focused work: 3 × 12 slow bicep curls or reverse curls with light load.** Straight-arm work destroys unprepared elbows. This 5 minutes is the cheapest injury insurance in the document.

## 7B. Advanced: Straight-Arm Strength & Skill Development

**Who it's for:** 10+ strict pull-ups, 15+ dips, solid freestanding handstand, some tuck front lever.
**Goal by Week 12:** advance one tier on front lever and planche, achieve or clean up the muscle-up, build toward handstand push-up / press.
**Frequency:** 5 days — 3 skill+strength, 2 accessory/hypertrophy.

### Straight-arm progression tiers

| Skill | Tiers (progress when you hold the current tier 3 × 10–12 s with clean form) |
|---|---|
| **Front lever** | Tuck → advanced tuck → one-leg → straddle → full |
| **Back lever** | Tuck → advanced tuck → straddle → full |
| **Planche** | Frog stand → tuck planche → advanced tuck → straddle → full |
| **Handstand push-up** | Wall HSPU → deficit wall HSPU → freestanding HSPU → 90° press |
| **Muscle-up** | High pull-to-sternum → explosive pull-up → jumping/banded MU → strict bar MU → ring MU |

### Weekly structure

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Pull skill day** — handstand 15 min · front lever 6×8–10 s · weighted pull-up 4×5 · ring row 3×10 · rear delt/scap 3×15 |
| Tue | **Push skill day** — planche 6×8–10 s · pseudo planche push-up 4×8 · ring dip (RTO) 4×6 · HSPU progression 4×5 · triceps 3×12 |
| Wed | **Legs + core** — pistol 4×5/side · Nordic curl 3×5 · jump squat 4×5 · dragon flag 4×5 · hanging leg raise 4×8 |
| Thu | **Muscle-up + power** — explosive pull 5×3 · MU progression 6×2 · straight-bar dip 4×6 · rope climb or towel hang |
| Fri | **Hypertrophy accessory** — weighted dip 4×8 · weighted chin 4×8 · ring fly 3×10 · face pull 4×15 · curls 3×12 · wrist/elbow circuit |
| Sat/Sun | One full rest day + one optional easy skill/mobility session |

### 12-week arc

| Wks | Skill sets | Intensity | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 5–6 × 8–10 s @ 70% max hold | Sub-max | Volume accumulation, tissue prep |
| **4** | 3 × 6 s | Easy | **Deload** — connective tissue needs this more than muscle does |
| 5–7 | 6–8 × 10–12 s @ 80% | Moderate | Add eccentric lowers and negatives from the tier above |
| **8** | 3 × 8 s | Easy | **Deload** |
| 9–11 | 4–6 × max-quality holds; attempt next tier | High | Test tier progressions weekly, one attempt each |
| 12 | 2–3 easy holds | Very light | **Deload + film max holds for the record** |

### Advanced principles
- **Rings > bar** for shoulder health and strength transfer. Add RTO (rings turned out) to dips and support holds.
- **Never chase two straight-arm skills to failure in one session.** Front lever and planche both tax the biceps tendon and anterior shoulder from opposite directions.
- **Weighted calisthenics is not optional at this level.** Weighted pull-ups and dips are what make the skill work possible — pure bodyweight ceilings out.
- **Film every skill attempt.** Your proprioception lies to you about hip height and elbow lockout.
- **If elbow pain appears, stop straight-arm work for 7–10 days** and load eccentrics (curls, reverse curls, wrist work) instead. Biceps tendinopathy from planche/lever rushing takes months, not days, to resolve.

# PART 2 — BODYBUILDING (HYPERTROPHY)

## The mechanics that actually drive growth

1. **Mechanical tension** through a full range of motion, especially the **lengthened** position, is the primary driver. Deep stretch under load beats short-range pump work.
2. **Proximity to failure** — sets taken to 0–4 RIR count as stimulating sets. Sets at 6+ RIR are mostly junk volume.
3. **Volume** — 10–20 hard sets per muscle group per week is the productive band for most people. More is not better past your recovery ceiling.
4. **Frequency** — hitting each muscle 2× per week beats 1× at equal volume.
5. **Progressive overload** — via **double progression**: hit the top of the rep range on all sets → add load → drop back to the bottom of the range.

## 2A. Beginner: Foundation Hypertrophy

**Who it's for:** <12 months of consistent lifting.
**Goal by Week 12:** clean technique on every core pattern, 10–20 lb added to main lifts, visible change in photos.
**Frequency:** Weeks 1–6 = 3 days full body. Weeks 7–12 = 4 days upper/lower.
**Intensity:** start at 4 RIR (Week 1) and progress to 1 RIR by Week 11. Beginners grow from *learning to train hard*, not from being buried.

### Weeks 1–6 — Full Body A/B/A, A/B/A alternating

**Day A**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | RIR |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet or barbell squat | 3 × 8–10 | 3 |
| Dumbbell bench press | 3 × 8–12 | 2–3 |
| Chest-supported row | 3 × 10–12 | 2 |
| Romanian deadlift | 2 × 10 | 3 |
| Lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 | 1 |
| Plank | 3 × 30–45 s | — |

**Day B**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | RIR |
|---|---|---|
| Trap bar or Romanian deadlift | 3 × 6–8 | 3 |
| Incline DB press | 3 × 8–12 | 2 |
| Lat pulldown | 3 × 10–12 | 2 |
| Leg press or split squat | 3 × 10–12 | 2 |
| Cable curl + triceps pushdown | 2 × 12 each | 1 |
| Hanging knee raise | 3 × 10 | — |

### Weeks 7–12 — Upper / Lower ×2

**Upper 1** Bench 4×6–8 · Row 4×8–10 · Incline DB 3×10–12 · Lat pulldown 3×10–12 · Lateral raise 3×15 · Curl 3×10
**Lower 1** Squat 4×6–8 · RDL 3×8–10 · Leg press 3×12 · Leg curl 3×12 · Calf raise 4×12 · Ab wheel 3×8
**Upper 2** Overhead press 4×6–8 · Pull-up/assisted 4×AMRAP · DB bench 3×10–12 · Cable row 3×12 · Rear delt fly 3×15 · Triceps 3×12
**Lower 2** Trap bar DL 3×5 · Bulgarian split squat 3×10/side · Hip thrust 3×12 · Leg extension 3×15 · Seated calf 4×15 · Side plank 3×30 s

### 12-week progression map

| Weeks | Sets/muscle/wk | RIR target | Load action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 10–12 | 3–4 | Establish technique; add reps only |
| **4** | 7 | 4 | **Deload** — same lifts, 2 sets each |
| 5–7 | 12–14 | 2–3 | Double progression, add load weekly |
| **8** | 8 | 4 | **Deload** |
| 9–11 | 14–16 | 1–2 | Push hardest here |
| 12 | 6 | 4 | **Deload + retest photos/lifts** |

### Beginner priorities in order
Technique > consistency > progressive overload > volume > exercise selection > everything else. The person who does the boring plan for 12 weeks beats the person who does the perfect plan for 5.

## 2B. Advanced: High-Volume Specialization Mesocycle

**Who it's for:** 3+ years training, knows their RIR honestly, has stalled on general programs.
**Goal by Week 12:** measurable growth in 1–2 lagging muscle groups while maintaining everything else.
**Frequency:** 5–6 days, Push/Pull/Legs ×2 or PPL + Upper/Lower hybrid.
**Method:** volume landmarks. Start at **MEV** (minimum effective volume), ramp toward **MAV** (maximum adaptive volume), stop before **MRV** (maximum recoverable), deload, repeat.

### Volume landmark table (sets per muscle per week)

| Muscle | MEV | MAV range | MRV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest | 8 | 12–20 | 22 |
| Back | 10 | 14–22 | 25 |
| Quads | 8 | 12–18 | 20 |
| Hamstrings | 6 | 10–16 | 18 |
| Delts (side) | 8 | 16–26 | 28 |
| Biceps | 8 | 14–20 | 24 |
| Triceps | 6 | 10–18 | 20 |
| Calves | 8 | 12–20 | 22 |

**Specialization rule:** you can only specialize by *de-specializing* something else. Push your 1–2 target muscles toward MRV and drop everything else to MEV maintenance. Trying to specialize everything is just overtraining with extra steps.

### The mesocycle structure

| Weeks | Block | Volume | RIR | Techniques |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Accumulation | MEV → MAV (+1–2 sets/muscle/wk) | 3 → 1 | Straight sets only, full ROM, controlled eccentrics |
| **5** | Deload | 50% sets, 60% load | 4–5 | — |
| 6–8 | Intensification | MAV, held flat | 1 → 0 | Add myo-reps, drop sets, lengthened partials on final set only |
| **9** | Micro-deload | 60% sets | 4 | — |
| 10–11 | Overreach | MAV +10% (approach MRV) | 0 | Last set of each exercise to failure |
| 12 | Deload + test | 40% sets | 4–5 | Photos, tape, strength check |

### Sample PPL split (advanced, chest + side delt specialization)

**Push 1 (heavy)** Incline barbell 4×5–7 @1RIR · Flat DB press 4×8–10 · Weighted dip 3×8 · Cable lateral raise 5×12–15 · Overhead cable triceps ext 4×10–12 · Lateral raise myo-rep 1×15+3×5
**Pull 1 (heavy)** Weighted pull-up 4×5–7 · Chest-supported row 4×8–10 · Half-kneeling lat pullover 3×12 · Rear delt cable fly 4×15 · Incline DB curl 3×10 · Hammer curl 3×12
**Legs 1** Squat 4×5–7 · RDL 3×8 · Leg press 3×12 · Seated leg curl 3×12 · Standing calf 5×10
**Push 2 (metabolic)** Machine chest press 4×10–12 · Incline DB fly 4×12–15 lengthened-biased · Deficit push-up 3×AMRAP · Lateral raise 5×15–20 · Triceps pushdown 4×12 drop-set final
**Pull 2 (metabolic)** Lat pulldown 4×10–12 · Seated cable row 4×12 · Straight-arm pulldown 3×15 · Face pull 4×20 · Preacher curl 4×12
**Legs 2** Hack squat 4×10–12 · Bulgarian split squat 3×10/side · Lying leg curl 4×12 · Leg extension 4×15 + partials · Seated calf 5×15

### Advanced execution rules
- **Log everything.** Load × reps × RIR per set. If you can't see the trend, you can't autoregulate.
- **Stimulus-to-fatigue ratio:** an exercise that makes you sore for 4 days and adds nothing to the log is a bad exercise for you, regardless of who else swears by it.
- **Rest 2–3 min on compounds, 60–90 s on isolation.** Cutting rest cuts stimulating reps.
- **Nutrition drives this program.** A surplus of 200–350 kcal with 0.8–1 g/lb protein and gaining 0.25–0.5% bodyweight per week. On maintenance you're doing a very hard maintenance program.

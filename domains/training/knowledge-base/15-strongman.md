# PART 15 — STRONGMAN & ODD-OBJECT

## Why it exists

Barbells are balanced, symmetrical, and predictable. Almost nothing in life is. Strongman trains **strength applied to awkward objects across distance and duration** — which is why it bridges powerlifting (max force) and hybrid (force under fatigue) better than anything else. Events last 15–75 seconds under near-maximal load, which is a distinct and largely untrained energy system.

## Event vocabulary

| Event | Trains | Beginner entry |
|---|---|---|
| **Log / axle press** | Overhead power, thick-grip clean, bracing | Empty log or axle, 5 × 5 |
| **Farmer's walk** | Grip, traps, gait under load | 50% BW/hand × 50 ft |
| **Yoke walk** | Whole-body rigidity, spinal loading | 75% BW × 50 ft |
| **Atlas stones** | Hip extension, back strength, awkward loading | Sandbag → light stone to lap |
| **Sandbag / keg carry & load** | Bear hug capacity, breathing under compression | 50–80 lb bag |
| **Sled push/drag & truck pull** | Horizontal force, leg drive, conditioning | 100 lb sled × 50 ft |
| **Deadlift variants** (axle, car, 18") | Max pull, grip | Standard deadlift first |

**Safety framing:** strongman punishes ego faster than any other modality here. The events are heavy, unstable, and often moving. **Learn every event with a load you could do for 10 reps before you ever go near a heavy one.**

## 15A. Beginner: Event Introduction

**Who it's for:** solid barbell base (roughly 1.5× BW deadlift, BW press) but new to implements.
**Goal by Week 12:** competent and safe on all six core events, ready to enter a novice competition.
**Frequency:** 3 days — 2 barbell strength + 1 event day. Event exposure grows as barbell volume tapers.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| **Day 1 — Press focus** | Log or axle press 5 × 5 · Incline bench 4 × 8 · Barbell row 4 × 8 · Triceps + rear delt 3 × 12 · Grip: plate pinch 3 × 30 s |
| **Day 2 — Pull focus** | Deadlift 5 × 3 @ 75–80% · Front squat 4 × 6 · RDL 3 × 8 · Sandbag to shoulder 5 × 3/side · Weighted carry 4 × 50 ft |
| **Day 3 — Event day** | Rotating medley (below) |

### Event day progression

| Wks | Event day |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Learn one event per week at 50% of eventual load. 4 × 50 ft farmer / 4 × 50 ft yoke / 5 × 3 stone-to-lap. Technique only. |
| **4** | **Deload** — light sled and carries only |
| 5–7 | Two events per session, moderate load. Farmer 5 × 60 ft @ 60% BW/hand · Yoke 4 × 60 ft @ 100% BW · Sandbag load 5 × 3 |
| **8** | **Deload** |
| 9–11 | Full medley. Time every event. Add a "contest simulation" in Wk 11 — 4 events back to back with 5 min rest. |
| 12 | **TEST: max log press, max deadlift, farmer's for distance, stone series** |

### Beginner priorities
- **Bracing is the whole skill.** Big breath into the belt, 360° pressure, ribs down. Every event fails at the trunk first.
- **Grip trains every session** — pinch, hold, thick-bar. Grip is the single most common limiter in a novice's first competition.
- **Yoke: short, fast steps. Do not stride.** A long stride under a yoke is how the load starts oscillating, and once it oscillates you're done.
- **Stones: hips, not arms.** Lap it, sit back, re-grip high, then extend. Tacky and sleeves once loads get real.

## 15B. Advanced: Contest Prep

**Who it's for:** competing at Week 12 with a known event list.
**Frequency:** 4 days — 2 max-effort barbell, 2 event days.
**Method:** conjugate-style. Max effort rotates, event work becomes progressively more contest-specific.

### Block map

| Wks | Block | Barbell | Events |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Base** | ME work up to a heavy 3–5 · Repetition method 4 × 8–10 | General: all events, moderate load, longer distances than contest |
| **4** | Deload | 70% for 3 × 5 | Light medley |
| 5–7 | **Strength** | ME to heavy single @ RPE 9 · Supplemental 5 × 5 @ 75–82% | Contest events only, 80–90% of contest weight, contest distances |
| **8** | Deload | | Technique only |
| 9–11 | **Specificity** | 2–3 × 2 @ 85–90%, volume cut hard | Contest weights, contest implements, timed. One full medley simulation Wk 10. |
| 12 | **Taper/contest** | Openers only | 2 light event touches Mon/Tue, **CONTEST Sat** |

### Weekly layout (Weeks 5–7)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | **ME Lower** — deadlift variant work to heavy single · Front squat 4 × 5 · RDL 3 × 8 · Heavy ab work |
| Tue | **Event 1** — Moving events: yoke, farmer's, sled, medley. Timed, contest distance. |
| Thu | **ME Upper** — log/axle press to heavy single · Close-grip bench 5 × 5 · Rows 4 × 8 · Triceps and rear delt volume |
| Sat | **Event 2** — Static/loading events: stones, sandbags, hold-for-time, deadlift for reps at contest weight |

### Contest-specific work
- **Train the exact implement, weight, and distance** by Week 9. Yoke at contest weight for contest distance. Log at contest weight for the actual rep target. Surprises on the platform are unrecoverable.
- **Rep events:** train to the contest time cap, not to a rep number. If it's 60 s of log press, do 60 s of log press.
- **Recovery is the constraint.** Yoke and stones carry enormous systemic and spinal cost. Two heavy event days per week is the ceiling for nearly everyone. Three is how people arrive at contests already broken.
- **Body armor accessories:** neck, upper back, biceps (stones and yoke tear biceps — heavy hammer curls and rows are prophylactic), and hamstrings.

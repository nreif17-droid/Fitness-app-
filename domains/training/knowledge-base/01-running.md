# PART 1 — RUNNING

## 1A. Beginner: Zero to a Continuous 5K

**Who it's for:** no consistent running history, or returning after 6+ months off.
**Goal by Week 12:** run 5K continuously and finish a timed 5K.
**Frequency:** 3 running days + 2 strength/cross days.
**Governing principle:** the aerobic system adapts in weeks; tendons, bone and fascia adapt in months. Almost every beginner injury is a connective-tissue timeline problem, not a fitness problem. This is why the ramp looks conservative — it is deliberate.

### The run/walk ladder

All running here is Z2 effort — **if you can't speak a full sentence, walk.** Pace is irrelevant. Effort is everything.

| Week | Session structure (×3/wk) | Total run time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 × (1 min run / 2 min walk) | 8 min | Feels absurdly easy. That is the point. |
| 2 | 8 × (90 s run / 2 min walk) | 12 min | |
| 3 | 7 × (2 min run / 90 s walk) | 14 min | Add 4 × 15 s strides after one session |
| **4** | **6 × (2 min run / 2 min walk)** | **12 min** | **Deload** |
| 5 | 6 × (3 min run / 90 s walk) | 18 min | |
| 6 | 5 × (4 min run / 90 s walk) | 20 min | |
| 7 | 4 × (6 min run / 90 s walk) | 24 min | |
| **8** | **4 × (4 min run / 2 min walk)** | **16 min** | **Deload** |
| 9 | 3 × (9 min run / 2 min walk) | 27 min | |
| 10 | 2 × (14 min run / 2 min walk) | 28 min | One session: 1 × 20 min continuous |
| 11 | 1 × 25 min continuous + 5 min walk | 25–30 min | Longest run of the block |
| 12 | Mon 20 min easy · Wed 3 × 3 min at goal pace · **Sat 5K TEST** | — | Taper week |

### Weekly template

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Run (ladder) |
| Tue | Strength A + 20 min walk |
| Wed | Run (ladder) |
| Thu | Rest or 30 min easy walk/bike |
| Fri | Strength B |
| Sat | Run (ladder — the longest of the three) |
| Sun | Full rest |

### Strength A / B (2× weekly, 30 min, RPE 7, 2–3 sets)

**A:** Goblet squat 3×10 · Romanian deadlift 3×8 · Push-up (any incline) 3×AMRAP−2 · Split squat 3×8/side · Dead bug 3×10/side
**B:** Step-up 3×10/side · Single-leg calf raise 3×12/side (critical for Achilles tolerance) · Inverted row 3×8 · Hip thrust 3×12 · Side plank 3×30 s/side

### Beginner rules that prevent 90% of failures

1. **Never increase weekly running volume more than ~10%.** The ladder above already respects this.
2. **Cadence ~170–180 steps/min.** Shorter, quicker steps drastically cut impact per stride. Count for 15 s × 4.
3. **Shoes:** get fitted, replace at 400–500 miles. Don't switch to minimalist mid-program.
4. **A skipped run is nothing. Two skipped weeks means back up one rung, don't jump forward.**
5. **Never do the "extra" run because you feel great.** Feeling great is what Week 9 is built from.

## 1B. Advanced: Half-Marathon / 10K Peak Block

**Who it's for:** 18+ months consistent running, currently holding 25–35 mi/wk comfortably, injury-free.
**Goal by Week 12:** PR at 10K or half-marathon.
**Frequency:** 6 days (5 run + 1 quality cross), 40 → 58 mi/wk peak.
**Distribution:** 80/20 polarized. Two hard sessions per week. Never three.

### Session vocabulary

- **E** — Easy, Z2, conversational. This is the bulk. Should feel almost embarrassing.
- **T** — Threshold, Z4, ~1-hr race effort, 10–12 s/mi slower than 10K pace. Continuous or cruise intervals with 1 min float.
- **I** — Intervals, Z5, 3K–5K pace, 3–5 min reps, equal-time jog recovery.
- **R** — Reps, faster than 5K pace, 200–400 m, full recovery. Neuromuscular, not aerobic.
- **LR** — Long run, Z2 with optional finish quality.
- **Strides** — 6–8 × 20 s relaxed acceleration to ~mile pace, full walk recovery. Ends easy days. Free speed, zero fatigue cost.

### The 12-week build

| Wk | Mileage | Tue (quality 1) | Thu (quality 2) | Sat/Sun long | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40 | 4 × 6 min T / 1 min float | 8 × 400 R @ mile pace | 12 mi E | Reintroduce |
| 2 | 44 | 5 × 6 min T | 6 × 800 I @ 5K, 400 jog | 13 mi E | |
| 3 | 48 | 2 × 15 min T / 3 min | 10 × 400 R | 14 mi E | Peak of Block 1 |
| **4** | **32** | 20 min T | strides only | 10 mi E | **Deload** |
| 5 | 48 | 3 × 10 min T / 2 min | 5 × 1000 I @ 5K, 3 min jog | 14 mi, last 3 @ HMP | Intensify |
| 6 | 52 | 2 × 20 min T / 3 min | 6 × 1000 I | 15 mi, last 4 @ HMP | |
| 7 | 55 | 6 × 1 mi @ HMP / 1 min | 4 × 1200 I @ 3K–5K | 16 mi progression | Peak volume |
| **8** | **38** | 2 × 10 min T | strides only | 11 mi E | **Deload** |
| 9 | 55 | 8 mi @ goal HM pace | 5 × 3 min I @ 3K, 2 min jog | 16 mi, last 6 @ HMP | Specificity |
| 10 | 58 | 10 mi @ goal HM pace | 3 × 2 mi @ 10K–HMP / 2 min | 17 mi steady | **Peak week** |
| 11 | 46 | 3 × 2 mi @ HMP / 90 s | 6 × 800 I | 13 mi E | Sharpen |
| 12 | 26 | 4 × 3 min @ HMP | 3 × 1 mi @ HMP (Wed) | **RACE Sat** | **Taper** |

*HMP = goal half-marathon pace. Adjust all paces off your Week 0 test using a VDOT/equivalent-performance calculator — do not guess.*

### Weekly layout

Mon E 6–8 mi + strides · Tue **quality 1** (2–3 mi WU, work, 2 mi CD) · Wed E 6–8 mi recovery pace · Thu **quality 2** · Fri E 5 mi or full rest · Sat **long run** · Sun E 5 mi or 60 min Z2 bike/swim.

### Supporting strength (2× weekly, 25 min, keep it heavy and low-volume)

Trap bar deadlift 3×4 @ RPE 7 · Bulgarian split squat 3×6/side · Single-leg calf raise 4×8 slow eccentric · Copenhagen plank 3×20 s/side · Pogo hops 3×20. **Lift on quality days, not easy days** — protect the easy days' easiness. Cut all plyometrics in Week 11–12.

### Taper science

Cut **volume 40–60%, keep intensity and frequency.** The classic error is resting completely and going flat. Weeks 11–12 should feel like you're holding a dog back on a leash. Sleep is the actual training in taper week.

# PART 3 — POWERLIFTING

## 3A. Beginner: Linear Progression + Technical Base

**Who it's for:** <18 months lifting, no established 1RMs, still adding weight to the bar most sessions.
**Goal by Week 12:** technically sound squat/bench/deadlift and a genuine first 1RM.
**Frequency:** 3 days (Mon/Wed/Fri).
**Method:** you are the only population who gets to add weight every session. Use it before you lose it — this window closes and never reopens.

### The core template

**Day 1** Squat 3×5 · Bench 3×5 · Barbell row 3×8 · Ab wheel 3×8
**Day 2** Squat 3×5 (light, 85% of Day 1) · Overhead press 3×5 · Deadlift 1×5 · Chin-up 3×AMRAP
**Day 3** Squat 3×5 · Bench 3×5 · Romanian deadlift 3×8 · Face pull 3×15

### Progression rules

| Lift | Weekly load increase |
|---|---|
| Squat | +5–10 lb per session (Weeks 1–5), +5 lb (6+) |
| Deadlift | +10 lb per session early, +5 lb from Week 5 |
| Bench | +2.5–5 lb per session |
| Overhead press | +2.5 lb per session |

**When you miss reps:** repeat the weight. Miss twice at the same weight → **reset to 90% and build back**, adding weight in smaller jumps. This is normal and expected around Weeks 7–10. It is not failure, it's the program working.

### 12-week arc

| Weeks | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Load is easy, technique is the entire job. Film every top set from a 45° angle. |
| 5–8 | Loads get real (RPE 7–8). First stalls appear on press/bench. Reset as needed. |
| 9–11 | Squat/deadlift now RPE 8–9. Drop squat to 2 heavy days + 1 light. Add a 5th set only if recovery allows. |
| 12 | **Test week.** Mon: work to a heavy squat single. Wed: bench single. Fri: deadlift single. |

### Technical checklist (worth more than any program)

- **Squat:** brace before you unrack, mid-foot bar path, knees track over toes, hit depth every rep — no exceptions, no ego weight.
- **Bench:** feet planted, upper back tight and retracted, moderate arch, bar to lower chest, elbows ~45–70°, touch-and-press without bouncing.
- **Deadlift:** bar over mid-foot, lats engaged ("bend the bar," "protect the armpits"), take the slack out of the bar before you pull, hips and shoulders rise together.

### Beginner accessory dose
Keep it to 3–4 accessory movements per session, 8–15 reps, RPE 7–8. Rows, chin-ups, face pulls, hamstring and ab work. Accessories build the engine that lets the main lifts keep climbing — they are not decoration.

## 3B. Advanced: Block Periodized Peaking Cycle

**Who it's for:** established 1RMs, 2+ years of structured lifting, competing or testing at Week 12.
**Frequency:** 4 days, squat 2×/wk, bench 3×/wk (2 heavy + 1 volume), deadlift 1–2×/wk.
**Method:** classic three-block sequence — hypertrophy → strength → peaking → taper. Percentages are of a *realistic current* 1RM, cross-checked with RPE. **When the % and the RPE disagree, obey the RPE.**

### Block map

| Block | Weeks | Intensity | Volume/main lift | Rep scheme | RPE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hypertrophy | 1–4 | 65–75% | 20–30 reps | 4–6 × 5–8 | 6–8 |
| Strength | 5–8 | 75–87.5% | 12–20 reps | 4–6 × 3–5 | 7–9 |
| Peaking | 9–11 | 87.5–100% | 6–12 reps | 3–5 × 1–3 | 8–9.5 |
| Taper/Test | 12 | 50–105% | 3–6 reps | openers, then max | — |

### Week-by-week main lift prescription

| Wk | Squat (heavy day) | Bench (heavy day) | Deadlift |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5×6 @ 67.5% | 5×8 @ 65% | 4×6 @ 65% |
| 2 | 5×6 @ 70% | 5×8 @ 67.5% | 4×6 @ 67.5% |
| 3 | 5×5 @ 72.5% | 5×6 @ 72.5% | 4×5 @ 70% |
| **4** | **3×5 @ 65%** | **3×5 @ 65%** | **2×5 @ 62.5%** — deload |
| 5 | 5×4 @ 77.5% | 5×5 @ 75% | 4×4 @ 75% |
| 6 | 5×4 @ 80% | 5×4 @ 80% | 4×3 @ 80% |
| 7 | 5×3 @ 82.5% | 5×3 @ 82.5% | 4×3 @ 82.5% |
| **8** | **3×3 @ 72.5%** | **3×3 @ 72.5%** | **2×3 @ 70%** — deload |
| 9 | 4×2 @ 87.5% | 5×2 @ 87.5% | 3×2 @ 87.5% |
| 10 | 4×1 @ 90% + 3×2 @ 85% | 4×2 @ 90% | 3×1 @ 90% |
| 11 | 2×1 @ 92.5–95% | 3×1 @ 92.5% | 2×1 @ 92.5% |
| 12 | Mon opener 88% ×1 · **Test/meet Sat** | | |

### Supplemental & variation work

Each main lift gets one **variation** (attacking your specific sticking point) plus accessories:

| Weakness | Squat fix | Bench fix | Deadlift fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Off the floor / out of the hole | Pause squat 3 s | Spoto press, larsen press | Deficit deadlift 1–2" |
| Mid-range | Tempo squat 4-0-1 | Close-grip bench | Snatch-grip deadlift |
| Lockout | Pin squat, front squat | Board press, floor press | Rack pull, banded lockout |
| Bracing/upper back | SSB squat, front squat | Heavy rowing | Paused deadlift below knee |

Variations run at 70–85% for 3–4 × 3–5, placed on the second session of the week. Fade them out entirely by Week 10 — peaking demands specificity, not variety.

### Peaking rules
- **Never test a true max in Weeks 1–8.** You're spending the adaptation you're supposed to be building.
- **Openers should be a weight you could triple.** Second attempt = a confident single you've hit in training. Third = a small PR.
- Deload the *supplemental* work first, main lifts second. Accessories drop to near zero by Week 11.
- **Bodyweight, food, and sleep get boring and consistent from Week 9 onward.** Peaking is a fatigue-management exercise, not a courage exercise.

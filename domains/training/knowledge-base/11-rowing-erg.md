# PART 11 — ROWING / ERG

## Why the erg

Full-body (roughly 60% legs, 30% trunk, 10% arms), near-zero impact, and precisely measurable. It's the best running substitute for anyone whose shins, knees, or Achilles are the limiter, and the best standalone conditioning modality for lifters — it builds a large aerobic base without the eccentric damage that interferes with strength work.

## Technique — the entire ballgame

**The drive sequence: legs → hips → arms.** The recovery reverses it: **arms → hips → legs.**

- **Catch:** shins vertical, shoulders in front of hips, arms straight, relaxed grip.
- **Drive:** push the floor away with the legs first — the handle should barely move while the legs do 60% of the work. Then swing the hips open, then finally pull the arms.
- **Finish:** handle at the bottom of the ribs, legs flat, slight backward lean (~11 o'clock), elbows past the body.
- **Recovery:** arms extend, hips hinge over, *then* the knees bend. Twice as slow as the drive.

**Ratio 1:2** — the recovery takes twice as long as the drive. This is what makes low stroke rates powerful instead of lazy.

**Common faults:** opening the back too early (shooting the tail), bending the arms at the catch, rushing the slide, gripping like a deadlift, and setting the damper to 10 (which is not "harder," it's just a bigger, slower boat — **damper 3–5 for most people, drag factor 110–130**).

## The metric: split

**Split** = the time to cover 500 m at your current pace. All work below is prescribed off your **2K split**.

**Test:** 2000 m all-out, after a 15-min warm-up with rate ladders. Brutal and definitive. Retest Week 12.

| Zone | Split relative to 2K pace | Rate (spm) |
|---|---|---|
| UT2 (base) | 2K + 18–22 s | 18–20 |
| UT1 (tempo) | 2K + 12–16 s | 20–24 |
| AT (threshold) | 2K + 8–10 s | 24–26 |
| TR (VO₂) | 2K + 4–6 s | 26–30 |
| AN (anaerobic) | 2K − 2 s and faster | 30–36 |

## 11A. Beginner: Technique & Aerobic Base

**Who it's for:** new to the erg, or has been yanking on it for years without structure.
**Goal by Week 12:** a clean, repeatable stroke, 10,000 m continuous, and a real 2K time.
**Frequency:** 3 sessions/wk.

| Wk | Session 1 (technique) | Session 2 (steady) | Session 3 (intervals) | Weekly m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 × 1 min drills, 1 min rest | 3 × 5 min UT2 | 8 × 250 m, 90 s rest | ~7,000 |
| 2 | Pause drill 6 × 3 min | 2 × 10 min UT2 | 8 × 500 m, 2 min rest | ~9,000 |
| 3 | Legs-only → full stroke, 6 × 4 min | 25 min continuous UT2 | 6 × 750 m, 2 min | ~11,000 |
| **4** | 20 min easy technique | 20 min UT2 | — | ~7,000 **deload** |
| 5 | Rate ladder 18/20/22, 6 × 3 min | 30 min UT2 | 5 × 1000 m, 3 min | ~13,000 |
| 6 | Feet-out rowing 5 × 4 min | 35 min UT2 | 8 × 500 m @ AT, 90 s | ~15,000 |
| 7 | Rate ladder to 24, 6 × 4 min | 40 min UT2 | 4 × 1500 m, 3 min | ~17,000 |
| **8** | 20 min technique | 25 min UT2 | — | ~9,000 **deload** |
| 9 | 5 × 5 min UT1 | 45 min UT2 | 6 × 750 m @ TR, 2 min | ~19,000 |
| 10 | 4 × 8 min UT1 | **10,000 m continuous** | 10 × 250 m @ AN, 90 s | ~22,000 |
| 11 | 3 × 10 min AT | 40 min UT2 | 4 × 500 m @ 2K pace, 3 min | ~18,000 |
| 12 | 20 min easy | 2 × 750 m sharp | **2000 m TEST** | ~9,000 **taper** |

### Beginner drills
- **Pause drill:** pause 2 s at arms-away and at hands-over-knees on every recovery. Teaches sequencing.
- **Legs-only → legs+body → full stroke:** 10 strokes each, build the stroke from parts.
- **Feet-out rowing:** straps off. If you fall backward at the finish or slam the catch, your sequencing is wrong. Ruthless and immediate feedback.

## 11B. Advanced: 2K Peak Block

**Who it's for:** established rower with a 2K under ~7:30 (men) / ~8:15 (women), rowing 5×/wk.
**Goal by Week 12:** 5–15 s off the 2K.
**Frequency:** 5–6 sessions, 60–90 km/wk.

| Wks | Block | Signature sessions | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **UT2 base** | 3 × 60–70 min steady @ r18–20; 1 × 4 × 2000 m UT1 | 60 → 70 km |
| **4** | Recovery | UT2 only, 60% volume | 40 km |
| 5–7 | **Threshold** | 4 × 2000 m @ AT r24, 4 min rest; 3 × 20 min @ UT1; 8 × 500 m @ TR r28 | 70 → 80 km |
| **8** | Recovery | UT2 + short rate work | 45 km |
| 9–11 | **Race specificity** | 5 × 1000 m @ 2K+2 r30, 5 min; 4 × 750 m @ 2K pace; 8 × 500 m @ 2K−1 r32; **broken 2K** (500/500/500/500, 1 min rest) | 75 → 55 km |
| 12 | **Taper** | 2 × 1000 m @ 2K+3; rate primers; **2K TEST** | 30 km |

### The two sessions that matter most
- **4 × 2000 m @ AT, r24, 4 min rest.** The single best erg session ever devised. If you can hold this at 2K+8, your 2K is where it should be.
- **Broken 2K** (Weeks 10–11): 4 × 500 m at goal 2K pace with 1 min rest. Teaches your body what goal pace feels like without the full cost. If you can't hold goal pace here, your goal is wrong.

### Race-day 2K strategy
First 500 m: 1–2 s faster than goal split (adrenaline, high rate ~34–36). Middle 1000: settle to goal split at r30–32 — **this is where the race is won or lost, and it feels awful by 1200 m going in**. Last 500: rate up progressively, sprint the final 250. Expect the middle thousand to be the hardest sustained thing you do all year.

### Supporting work
2 lifting sessions weekly — squat, deadlift, row, press at 3–5 × 5, RPE 7–8. Rowers need posterior chain and a spine that tolerates 240 flexion-extension cycles at load. Add 3 × 45 s side planks and back extensions every session.

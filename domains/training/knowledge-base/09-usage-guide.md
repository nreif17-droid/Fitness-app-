# PART 9 — HOW TO USE THIS LIBRARY

### Choosing a program
1. **Pick one primary modality per 12-week block.** Two primaries means two mediocre outcomes.
2. **Choose the level honestly.** If in doubt, take the beginner track — the cost of running beginner programming when you're intermediate is 12 slow weeks. The cost of the reverse is injury and a lost quarter.
3. **A second modality can run at maintenance dose alongside** — 2 sessions/week at moderate intensity holds nearly all of an existing quality.

### Legal pairings for a single 12-week block

| Primary | Good maintenance partner | Avoid pairing with |
|---|---|---|
| Powerlifting | Calisthenics (upper), Z2 cycling | High-mileage running |
| Bodybuilding | Z2 cycling/walking, swimming | Advanced hybrid |
| Running | Powerlifting-lite (2×/wk), swimming | Bodybuilding overreach block |
| Cycling | Calisthenics, light lifting | Advanced running |
| Swimming | Calisthenics, cycling | Heavy pressing volume |
| Calisthenics | Running, cycling | Advanced powerlifting peak |
| Hybrid | (already the combination) | Anything additional |

### Sequencing across a year
Four 12-week blocks per year with 1 transition week between each:
**Q1 Base/hypertrophy → Q2 Strength → Q3 Specificity/peak → Q4 General/repair.** Rotate which modality gets the peak each year. This is how you build a decade of progress instead of three good months and a plateau.

### The tracking minimum
Per session: date, sessions completed vs planned, key numbers (load × reps × RIR, or distance × pace × HR), sleep hours, and a 1–10 readiness score taken *before* training. Weekly: total volume, bodyweight average, one sentence on how the week felt. That's it — anything more gets abandoned by Week 5.

### The honest expectation setting
- **Beginners** will see 15–40% improvement in most tested metrics over 12 weeks. This will never happen this fast again.
- **Advanced athletes** should expect 2–7%. A 5% FTP gain or a 10 lb squat PR at high training age is a *good* block.
- **The programs are not the variable that determines your outcome.** Adherence, sleep, and food are. A B-grade program executed at 90% adherence beats an A+ program at 60% adherence, every single time.

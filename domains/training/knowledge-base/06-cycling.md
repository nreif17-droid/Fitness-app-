# PART 6 — CYCLING

## The metric that governs everything: FTP

**FTP** = functional threshold power, roughly the power you can hold for ~40–60 minutes. All zones below are % of FTP.

**Test protocol:** 20 min WU with 3 × 1 min openers → 5 min all-out (to clear) → 10 min easy → **20 min max sustainable effort** → FTP = avg power × 0.95. Retest Week 12 identically.

| Zone | %FTP | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 Recovery | <55% | Flush | 30–60 min |
| Z2 Endurance | 56–75% | Aerobic base, fat oxidation, capillarization | 1.5–5 h |
| Z3 Tempo | 76–87% | Muscular endurance | 20–60 min |
| **Sweet spot** | 88–94% | Best adaptation-per-fatigue ratio | 2 × 20 to 3 × 20 |
| Z4 Threshold | 95–105% | Lactate clearance | 8–30 min reps |
| Z5 VO₂max | 106–120% | Ceiling | 3–5 min reps |
| Z6 Anaerobic | 121–150% | Repeatability | 30 s–2 min |

## 6A. Beginner: Aerobic Base & First Century Trajectory

**Who it's for:** rides occasionally, no structured training, no power meter necessary (use HR or RPE).
**Goal by Week 12:** ride 3 h continuously, establish an FTP, complete a 60-mile ride.
**Frequency:** 3–4 rides/wk, 4–8 h total.

### Weekly template

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Tue | 60 min Z2 + cadence drills (see below) |
| Thu | 60–75 min with tempo blocks |
| Sat | **Long ride** — the progression driver |
| Sun | 45–60 min Z1 recovery spin (optional) |

### Long ride progression

| Wk | Sat long ride | Thu quality |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 min Z2 | 3 × 5 min Z3 |
| 2 | 75 min Z2 | 3 × 6 min Z3 |
| 3 | 90 min Z2 | 4 × 6 min Z3 |
| **4** | 60 min Z2 | easy spin — **deload** |
| 5 | 105 min Z2 | 3 × 10 min Z3 |
| 6 | 2 h Z2 | 2 × 15 min sweet spot |
| 7 | 2.5 h Z2 | 3 × 12 min sweet spot |
| **8** | 90 min Z2 | 2 × 8 min Z3 — **deload** |
| 9 | 3 h Z2 | 2 × 20 min sweet spot |
| 10 | 3.5 h with 2 × 20 min Z3 | 4 × 8 min Z4 |
| 11 | **60-mile / 4 h ride** | 2 × 15 min sweet spot |
| 12 | 90 min easy | **FTP test** |

### Skills to build in Weeks 1–4 (they compound forever)
- **Cadence drills:** 3 × 5 min at 100–110 rpm in an easy gear, then 3 × 3 min at 60 rpm seated (torque work). Target a natural cadence of 85–95.
- **Fit check:** get a basic bike fit before Week 4. Knee pain, numb hands, and neck pain at this level are almost always fit problems, not toughness problems.
- **Fueling:** 60 g carbs/hr past the 90-minute mark, and drink before you're thirsty. Bonking once teaches this permanently; better to just believe it now.
- **Group riding, braking, and cornering** — practice deliberately, not accidentally at speed.

## 6B. Advanced: FTP Build & Race Peak

**Who it's for:** structured rider with a known FTP, 8–14 h/wk available.
**Goal by Week 12:** +5–10% FTP and a peaked event performance.
**Frequency:** 5–6 rides, 10–14 h.

### Block structure

| Wks | Block | Primary work | Weekly TSS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Sweet spot base** | 2 × 20 → 3 × 20 @ 88–94% | 550 → 700 |
| **4** | Recovery | Z2 only | 350 |
| 5–7 | **Threshold** | 3 × 15 @ 98–102%; over-unders | 700 → 800 |
| **8** | Recovery | Z2 + openers | 400 |
| 9–11 | **VO₂ + specificity** | 5 × 4 @ 110–118%; race simulation | 800 → 650 |
| 12 | **Taper** | Volume −50%, intensity retained | 350 |

### Key sessions in detail

**Sweet spot** — 3 × 20 min @ 90% FTP, 5 min recovery. The highest-yield session in cycling for time-crunched athletes. Ride these in the aero position if you race TT/tri.

**Over-unders** — 4 × 10 min alternating 2 min @ 95% / 1 min @ 105%. Trains lactate shuttling. Brutal and specific to real racing.

**VO₂max** — 5 × 4 min @ 112% FTP, 4 min recovery. Progress by *adding a rep, then extending to 5 min*, not by adding watts. If you can't hold target power on rep 4, the session's over — junk reps train nothing.

**Anaerobic repeatability** (Weeks 9–11, racers only) — 3 sets of 6 × 30 s @ 150% / 30 s easy, 8 min between sets.

**Long endurance** — 3.5–5 h Z2 with 3 × 20 min tempo inserted in the back half. Fatigue-resistance is trained by doing quality *late* in a ride, not fresh.

### Weekly layout (Weeks 5–7 example)
Mon rest/45 min Z1 · Tue over-unders 4 × 10 · Wed 2 h Z2 · Thu threshold 3 × 15 · Fri 90 min Z2 with cadence work · Sat 4 h endurance + tempo blocks · Sun 2 h Z2

### Ramp rate discipline
Increase weekly TSS by **no more than 5–8%**. Chronic training load rising faster than that is the most reliable predictor of illness and burnout in cyclists. Track CTL/ATL/TSB if you have the software — aim for TSB between −10 and −30 during build, and **+5 to +15 on event day**.

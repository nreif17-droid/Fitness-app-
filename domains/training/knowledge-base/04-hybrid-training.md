# PART 4 — HYBRID TRAINING

## The central problem: the interference effect

Concurrent strength and endurance training partially blunt each other. Endurance work activates AMPK (energy-sensing, mitochondrial); strength work activates mTOR (growth). Run AMPK hard immediately before a lift and you suppress the growth signal.

**The four rules that manage it:**

1. **Separate modalities by 6+ hours** when in the same day, or put them on different days entirely.
2. **If they must be back-to-back, lift first** (unless the endurance quality is the block's priority).
3. **Lower body endurance interferes with lower body strength most.** Cycling and rowing interfere less with running-specific strength than more running does.
4. **Feed the work.** Hybrid athletes chronically under-eat. Carbohydrate is not optional here.

## 4A. Beginner: General Physical Preparedness Base

**Who it's for:** wants to be broadly capable — can lift, can run, can carry, can go all day. Not chasing a single number.
**Goal by Week 12:** bodyweight bench, 1.5× bodyweight deadlift trajectory, run 5K under 30 min, complete a 45-min mixed effort without falling apart.
**Frequency:** 5 days — 2 strength, 2 conditioning, 1 mixed.

### Weekly template

| Day | Session | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Strength A** | Squat 3×5 · Bench 3×5 · Row 3×8 · Farmer carry 3×40 yd |
| Tue | **Z2 aerobic** | 30–45 min run/bike/row, nose-breathing pace |
| Wed | **Strength B** | Deadlift 3×5 · Overhead press 3×5 · Chin-up 3×AMRAP · Hanging leg raise 3×10 |
| Thu | **Intervals** | 6–10 × 1 min hard / 2 min easy (bike or row — lower impact) |
| Fri | Rest or 30 min walk | |
| Sat | **Mixed effort** | See ladder below |
| Sun | Full rest or 45 min Z1 hike | |

### Saturday mixed-effort ladder

| Wks | Session |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 20 min AMRAP: 400 m run · 10 kettlebell swings · 10 push-ups · 10 air squats |
| **4** | Deload: 20 min easy Z2 only |
| 5–7 | 30 min: 5 rounds of 600 m run · 15 swings · 10 goblet squats · 8 burpees |
| **8** | Deload |
| 9–11 | 40 min: 1 mi run · 30 swings · 20 push-ups · 400 m farmer carry · repeat |
| 12 | **Test week** (see below) |

### Strength progression
Add 5 lb to lower-body lifts and 2.5 lb to upper-body lifts weekly while all reps stay clean at RPE ≤8. Deload weeks 4, 8: drop to 2 sets at 70%.

### Conditioning progression
Z2 sessions grow 5 min/week to a 60-min ceiling. Intervals grow one rep per week to a 10-rep ceiling, then extend the work interval to 90 s and reset to 6 reps.

### Week 12 test battery
Mon: 3-lift max-effort triples. Wed: 5K time trial. Fri: max strict pull-ups + max push-ups in 2 min + 800 m farmer carry for time.

## 4B. Advanced: Hybrid Performance Block (Hyrox / Tactical / Ultra-Strength)

**Who it's for:** already squats ~1.75× bodyweight and runs sub-22 5K, wants both to climb.
**Goal by Week 12:** hold or add strength while significantly moving aerobic performance — e.g. **sub-20 5K + 2× bodyweight deadlift**, or a competitive Hyrox/Deka finish.
**Frequency:** 6 days, 8–9 sessions (3 doubles). ~8–10 h/wk.

### Weekly architecture

| Day | AM | PM |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Lower strength** — squat/DL main work | 30 min Z2 recovery bike |
| Tue | **Threshold run** — 3–5 × 6–8 min @ Z4 | — |
| Wed | **Upper strength** — press/pull main work | 40 min Z2 |
| Thu | **VO₂ / erg intervals** — 5 × 3 min @ Z5 | Mobility 20 min |
| Fri | **Full-body power + carries** | — |
| Sat | **Long aerobic** 75–110 min Z2 (run or run/bike brick) | — |
| Sun | Rest or 45 min Z1 | — |

### 12-week block sequencing

| Wks | Strength emphasis | Aerobic emphasis | Weekly hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Hypertrophy 4×6–8 @ 70–75% | Volume build, Z2 heavy, one threshold | 8 |
| **4** | 2×5 @ 65% | −40% volume | 5 |
| 5–7 | Strength 5×3–5 @ 80–87.5% | Threshold + first VO₂ work | 9 |
| **8** | 3×3 @ 72.5% | −30% | 6 |
| 9–11 | **Maintenance:** 2×3 @ 85–90%, 2 lifts/wk only | Race specificity, compromised-running, event simulation | 10 → 8 |
| 12 | 1 primer session | Taper 50%, **test/race** | 4 |

**The key strategic insight:** in Weeks 9–11 strength shifts to *maintenance dose* — you can hold strength on ~2 heavy sessions per week at 85%+ for 2–3 sets. That freed recovery goes entirely into aerobic specificity. Trying to build both maximally in the same 3 weeks is how hybrid athletes stall out at mediocre in both.

### Event-specific work (Hyrox-style)

Compromised running is the whole sport. Insert weekly from Week 5:
- **Brick:** 1 km run @ race pace → sled push 50 m → 1 km run → sled pull 50 m → repeat ×4
- **Grip endurance:** farmer carry 2 × 200 m at 50% bodyweight per hand, plus dead hangs 3 × max
- **Wall balls & burpee broad jumps:** train these to *pacing*, not to failure. 100 wall balls broken 25/25/25/25 with 20 s rest beats a heroic 60 followed by a collapse.
- **Ski erg / row:** 4 × 1000 m at target split, 2 min rest

### Fueling for hybrid
3–5 g carbohydrate per lb bodyweight is too much for most, but 1.5–2.5 g/lb on high-volume weeks is realistic. 90 g carbs/hr for sessions over 90 min. Under-fueling shows up first as loss of top-end power and mood, then as injury.

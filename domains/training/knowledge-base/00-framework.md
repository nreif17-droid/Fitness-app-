# The 12-Week Training Module Library

**Seven modalities × two levels = 14 complete programs.**
Running · Bodybuilding · Powerlifting · Hybrid · Swimming · Cycling · Calisthenics

---

## PART 0 — THE UNIVERSAL FRAMEWORK

Every program in this library runs on the same 12-week skeleton. Learn the skeleton once and all fourteen programs become readable.

### The macro-shape

| Block | Weeks | Job | Volume | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Block 1 — Accumulate** | 1–4 | Build work capacity and tissue tolerance | High, climbing | Low–moderate |
| **Block 2 — Intensify** | 5–8 | Convert capacity into quality | Moderate | Moderate–high |
| **Block 3 — Realize** | 9–11 | Express the adaptation at goal specificity | Low–moderate | High |
| **Week 12 — Taper & Test** | 12 | Shed fatigue, measure the result | Very low | Sharp but brief |

**Deload rule:** Week 4 = light (−40% volume, intensity held). Week 8 = moderate (−30% volume, −10% intensity). Week 12 = taper (−50 to −60% volume, intensity held or raised briefly). Beginners run 3:1 (three loading weeks, one deload). Advanced athletes with high training age can run 4:1 or 5:1 in Block 1 and tighten to 2:1 by Block 3.

### The five progression levers

You only ever get stronger/faster by turning one of these. Turn **one at a time**:

1. **Load** — more weight, more watts, faster pace
2. **Volume** — more sets, more reps, more distance
3. **Density** — same work in less time (shorter rest)
4. **Complexity** — harder movement variant, harder terrain, harder skill
5. **Frequency** — more sessions per week

Beginners should turn levers in that order and stall out on load/volume for a long time before touching density. Advanced athletes usually have only complexity and density left.

### Autoregulation — RPE and RIR

- **RIR** = Reps In Reserve. "3 RIR" = you could have done 3 more.
- **RPE** = 10 − RIR. RPE 7 = 3 reps left. RPE 10 = failure.
- **Endurance RPE** uses a talk test: Z1–2 = full sentences. Z3 = short sentences. Z4 = a few words. Z5 = one word or nothing.

Every prescription below has a target RPE. **If the prescribed load feels 2+ RPE points harder than the target for two sessions running, you are under-recovered — take the deload early.** The calendar serves you, not the other way around.

### The zone model (used in running, cycling, swimming, hybrid)

| Zone | %Max HR | %Threshold pace/power | Feel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 | 50–60% | <75% | Very easy | Recovery, flushing |
| Z2 | 60–70% | 75–88% | Conversational, nose-breathing | Aerobic base, mitochondria, fat oxidation |
| Z3 | 70–80% | 88–95% | "Comfortably hard" | Tempo — useful but easy to overuse |
| Z4 | 80–90% | 95–105% | Threshold, 1-hr race effort | Lactate clearance |
| Z5 | 90–100% | >105% | VO₂max intervals | Ceiling raising |

**The 80/20 rule:** roughly 80% of your weekly endurance minutes live in Z1–Z2, 20% in Z3–Z5. The single most common mistake in endurance training is running/riding the easy days too hard and the hard days too easy.

### Testing week protocol (Week 0 and Week 12)

Do **not** start any program without a baseline. Week 0 (or Day 1–3 of Week 1) tests, Week 12 retests identically — same conditions, same time of day, same warm-up.

| Modality | Test |
|---|---|
| Running | 5K time trial (beginner: 1-mile or 12-min Cooper test) |
| Bodybuilding | Photos (4 angles, same light), tape at 7 sites, bodyweight avg over 7 days, 3 key lifts for reps |
| Powerlifting | 1RM or 3RM squat/bench/deadlift, or an @8 single |
| Hybrid | 3-lift total + 5K time + max pull-ups, on separate days |
| Swimming | 400m TT and 200m TT (gives you CSS — see swim section) |
| Cycling | 20-min FTP test (×0.95) or ramp test |
| Calisthenics | Max strict pull-ups, push-ups, dips, longest hold of your current top skill |

### The non-negotiable layer (all 14 programs)

- **Sleep 7–9 h.** This is the highest-leverage variable in the document. Nothing below matters if this is broken.
- **Protein 0.7–1.0 g per lb bodyweight** daily on any strength/hypertrophy program; 0.6–0.8 on endurance blocks.
- **Warm-up, every session:** 5 min easy aerobic → 5 min dynamic mobility for the day's joints → 2–3 ramp sets or strides at target intensity. Non-optional in Blocks 2–3.
- **Hard days hard, easy days easy.** No medium mush.
- **Two consecutive nights of poor sleep or a resting HR 7+ bpm above baseline = swap the hard session for Z2 or rest.**

### Red-flag stop list

Stop and reassess (or see a professional) for: sharp joint pain vs. muscular burn; pain that worsens through a warm-up rather than easing; numbness or tingling down a limb; chest pain or unusual shortness of breath; bone-point tenderness you can cover with one fingertip (possible stress reaction); resting HR elevated 10+ bpm for 3+ days; loss of appetite plus disturbed sleep plus mood crash together (classic overreaching triad).
